Family Planning & Contraception

Family planning helps couples decide when to have children and how to space pregnancies. There are many safe and effective methods, and the right one depends on your health, plans and preferences.

About Family Planning & Contraception

Spacing pregnancies at least two years apart gives the mother’s body time to recover and is healthier for both mother and baby. Family planning also helps couples plan around education, work and finances.

Dr. Jawaria Rasheed provides private, respectful family planning advice in Bahawalpur, helping couples compare methods honestly and choose what fits them best.

When to seek advice

  • ✓After childbirth, to space the next pregnancy
  • ✓Choosing a method for the first time
  • ✓Side effects or problems with your current method
  • ✓Planning to stop contraception and conceive
  • ✓Wanting a long-acting or permanent method

Before choosing a method

  • Health review — blood pressure, migraine, smoking and medical conditions.
  • Your plans — when you might want another child.
  • Preferences — daily, monthly or long-acting methods.
  • Breastfeeding — some methods are preferred while breastfeeding.

Methods we can discuss

Copper IUCD (coil)

Long-acting, hormone-free and reversible; effective for several years.

Hormonal IUD

Long-acting; also reduces heavy periods.

Implant

A small rod under the skin of the arm, effective for several years.

Injection

Given every few months.

Pills

Combined or progestogen-only pills taken daily.

Condoms

Also protect against infections.

Permanent methods

Tubal ligation for couples who have completed their family.

After starting a method

A follow-up visit helps check that the method suits you. Contact the clinic if you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever or a missed period.

Contraception after childbirth

Fertility can return sooner than expected after delivery, even before periods restart. Breastfeeding alone is reliable only under specific conditions. Progestogen-only methods, the copper coil and implants are suitable for breastfeeding mothers. Discuss your plan during pregnancy or soon after delivery.

Choosing the right method

The best method is the one you can use correctly and comfortably. Long-acting methods such as the coil and implant are the most effective because they do not depend on remembering a pill. Some methods offer extra benefits — for example, the hormonal IUD reduces heavy periods. Your doctor will check that the method is safe with your health conditions.

Myths and facts

  • Myth: the coil causes infertility. Fact: fertility returns soon after removal.
  • Myth: pills cause cancer. Fact: combined pills actually lower the risk of ovarian and endometrial cancer.
  • Myth: you need a break from the pill. Fact: breaks are not needed and increase the chance of unplanned pregnancy.

Frequently asked questions

Will contraception affect my future fertility?

For most methods, fertility returns soon after stopping. The injection may take a few months longer.

When can I start contraception after delivery?

Some methods can be started soon after delivery; your doctor will advise based on breastfeeding and type of delivery.

Is the coil painful to insert?

Most women feel short cramping. It takes only a few minutes.

Do pills cause weight gain?

Most studies show little or no weight gain with modern pills.

Is emergency contraception available?

Yes. It works best when used as soon as possible after unprotected intercourse. Contact the clinic for advice.
