Pap Smear & Cervical Screening

A Pap smear is a simple test that collects a few cells from the cervix to look for early changes. Finding changes early means they can be treated before they become serious.

Book an appointmentWhatsAppCall 0300 0000000

Who may need this?

  • ✓Routine screening for sexually active women, as advised by your doctor
  • ✓Abnormal bleeding or discharge (needs examination, not just screening)
  • ✓Follow-up after an earlier abnormal result

What to expect

  • A short examination in privacy with a female doctor
  • The test takes a few minutes and may feel slightly uncomfortable
  • Results and next steps explained to you

Try to book when you are not on your period. Avoid intercourse, vaginal creams or douching for two days before the test.

How often should I have a Pap smear?

It depends on your age and previous results. Your doctor will advise a schedule that suits you.
