Period Problems

Periods that are very heavy, painful, irregular or missed are common, but they are not something you simply have to live with. Finding the cause allows effective treatment.

Book an appointmentWhatsAppCall 0300 0000000

Who may need this?

  • ✓Bleeding that soaks through pads quickly or lasts more than 7 days
  • ✓Pain that stops you from daily activities
  • ✓Cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days
  • ✓Bleeding between periods or after intercourse
  • ✓Bleeding after menopause

What to expect

  • Questions about your cycle (a period diary helps)
  • Blood tests, for example for anaemia and thyroid
  • Pelvic ultrasound
  • Treatment options, from medicines to procedures if needed

Any bleeding after menopause should always be checked promptly.

Should I track my periods before the visit?

Yes. Note the start and end dates and how heavy each day is — a phone app or notebook is fine.
