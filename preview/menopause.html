Menopause Care

Menopause is a natural stage of life, but the years around it can bring hot flushes, night sweats, poor sleep, mood changes and vaginal dryness. Support is available.

Who may need this?

  • ✓Hot flushes or night sweats affecting daily life
  • ✓Irregular periods in your 40s
  • ✓Vaginal dryness or discomfort
  • ✓Questions about bone health and staying healthy after menopause

What to expect

  • Discussion of your symptoms and health risks
  • Advice on lifestyle, diet and exercise
  • Treatment options, including hormonal and non-hormonal choices
  • Screening advice suited to your age
