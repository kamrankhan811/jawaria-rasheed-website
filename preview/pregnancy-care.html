Pregnancy & Antenatal Care

Antenatal care means regular check-ups during pregnancy to look after the health of the mother and baby. Good care helps identify problems early and prepares you for a safe delivery.

Book an appointmentWhatsAppCall 0300 0000000

Who may need this?

  • ✓You have a positive pregnancy test
  • ✓You are planning a pregnancy and want advice beforehand
  • ✓You had complications in a previous pregnancy
  • ✓You have diabetes, high blood pressure, thyroid or another condition

What to expect

  • Confirmation of pregnancy and expected due date
  • Routine blood tests and ultrasound scans at the right stages
  • Blood pressure, weight and baby’s growth checks
  • Advice on diet, supplements, activity and warning signs
  • Planning for delivery, with referral where needed

Most check-ups are monthly in early pregnancy and become more frequent towards the end. Your schedule will be adjusted to your health and your baby’s needs.

When to seek urgent care: Contact a hospital emergency department straight away if you have heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, fits, severe headache with blurred vision, leaking fluid, or your baby’s movements reduce.

Common questions

When should I have my first antenatal visit?

Ideally soon after a positive pregnancy test, usually in the first 8–10 weeks.

Which supplements should I take?

Folic acid is usually advised before and in early pregnancy. Your doctor will advise on iron, calcium and other supplements based on your tests.
