Gynecology Consultation

A gynecology consultation is a private appointment to talk about any concern related to your periods, reproductive organs, hormones, or general women’s health. You will be listened to carefully and examined only with your consent.

Book an appointmentWhatsAppCall 0300 0000000

Who may need this?

  • ✓Irregular, painful or very heavy periods
  • ✓Vaginal discharge, itching or discomfort
  • ✓Lower abdominal or pelvic pain
  • ✓Questions about hormones, contraception or planning a pregnancy
  • ✓A routine women’s health check-up

What to expect

  • A conversation about your symptoms and medical history
  • An examination, only if needed and with your permission
  • Ultrasound or lab tests where they will help
  • A clear explanation of the findings and your options

Many women delay seeing a gynecologist because of hesitation or a busy schedule. A short visit can often explain symptoms that have been worrying you for months, and early advice usually means simpler treatment.

You are welcome to bring a family member with you. Please bring any previous reports, ultrasound scans and a list of medicines you take.

Common questions

Do I need to prepare for my first visit?

No special preparation is needed. Note the date of your last period and bring previous reports if you have them.

Can I come during my period?

Yes, for most consultations. If a specific test is planned, the clinic will tell you the best day to come.

This page is general information and does not replace a consultation. Your treatment plan depends on your individual examination and test results.
