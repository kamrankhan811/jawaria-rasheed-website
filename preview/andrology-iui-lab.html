What the lab does

Male factors play a part in many couples’ difficulty conceiving, so a fertility check always looks at both partners. The clinic’s andrology lab tests the semen sample and prepares sperm for IUI, and Dr. Jawaria explains every report to you in person.

  • ✓Semen analysis (count, movement and shape of sperm)
  • ✓Repeat and follow-up semen tests
  • ✓Sperm washing and preparation for IUI
  • ✓Same-day IUI with the prepared sample
  • ✓Reports explained clearly by the doctor

Semen analysis

A semen analysis is a simple, painless test. The lab looks at:

  • Volume — how much semen there is
  • Sperm concentration — how many sperm there are per millilitre
  • Motility — how well the sperm move
  • Morphology — the shape of the sperm
  • Other checks when needed, such as vitality or signs of infection

Results are compared with the reference values in the World Health Organization (WHO) laboratory manual for semen examination. Sperm quality naturally changes from one sample to the next, so one abnormal result does not mean infertility — the test is usually repeated after a few weeks before any decision is made.

How to prepare for the test

  • Avoid ejaculation for 2 to 7 days before the test.
  • The sample is collected by masturbation into a clean container given by the lab, in a private room at the clinic.
  • Do not use lubricants, saliva or a condom — they can harm the sperm.
  • Collect the whole sample; if any part is lost, tell the lab staff.
  • Tell the doctor about any fever or illness in the last 3 months and any medicines you take, as these can affect the result.
  • If you were asked to collect the sample at home, bring it to the lab within about an hour, kept close to body temperature. Please ask the clinic first.

Sperm preparation for IUI

On the day of IUI, the husband gives a fresh sample at the clinic. In the lab, the sample is washed and concentrated: the seminal fluid is removed and the healthiest, most active sperm are separated into a small volume. Dr. Jawaria then places this prepared sample directly into the uterus with a thin, soft catheter. Preparation takes a short while, so couples usually wait at the clinic.

Your IUI day, step by step

1. Fertility check

Both partners are assessed, including a semen analysis.

2. Follicle monitoring

Ultrasound scans track the growing egg follicle.

3. Timing

IUI is planned for the time of ovulation.

4. Sample & preparation

A fresh sample is given and prepared in the lab.

5. IUI

The prepared sperm are placed in the uterus. It takes a few minutes.

6. Pregnancy test

A test is done about two weeks later, as advised.

Privacy and safety

Every sample is labelled and checked against the couple’s identity at each step. Collection is private, and results are shared only with you and your doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Is a semen analysis painful?

No. The sample is collected privately by the man himself. There is no needle or procedure.

Does one abnormal report mean we cannot have a baby?

No. Sperm results vary from sample to sample. The test is usually repeated, and many couples with abnormal results still conceive with the right treatment.

How long should I abstain before the test?

Usually 2 to 7 days without ejaculation. The clinic will confirm what is right for you.

When will I get the report?

The clinic will tell you when your report is ready, and Dr. Jawaria will explain it to you.

Does the husband need to come on the IUI day?

Yes, in most cases, because a fresh sample is needed for sperm preparation shortly before the IUI.
