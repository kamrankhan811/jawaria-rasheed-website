Patient guide

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about appointments and care.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online request form, call the clinic or send a WhatsApp message. The clinic will confirm your date and time.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request. Your appointment is confirmed when the clinic contacts you.

Can my husband or a family member come with me?

Yes. For fertility consultations it is especially helpful if both partners attend.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Previous reports and scans, a list of your medicines and the date of your last period.

Is IUI the same as IVF?

No. In IUI, prepared sperm are placed in the uterus and fertilisation happens naturally inside the body. In IVF, eggs are collected and fertilised in a laboratory. IUI is simpler and less invasive. IVF may be advised in other situations.

Are consultations private?

Yes. Your concerns are discussed confidentially, and examinations are done only with your consent.

Do you provide emergency care?

No. For heavy bleeding, severe pain or other emergencies, please go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
