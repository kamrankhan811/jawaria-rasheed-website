IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)

Intrauterine insemination (IUI) is a fertility treatment in which washed and prepared sperm are placed inside the uterus around the time of ovulation. It brings healthy sperm closer to the egg, is quick and usually painless, and is far less invasive than IVF.

About IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)

In natural conception, only a small fraction of sperm travel from the vagina through the cervix and into the uterus and tubes. IUI bypasses the cervix and delivers a concentrated sample of the most active sperm right where they are needed, at the moment the egg is released.

IUI is often the first fertility treatment for couples who have been trying for a year or more, when the woman has at least one open fallopian tube and the semen sample is reasonably healthy. Dr. Jawaria Rasheed offers IUI in Bahawalpur as part of a complete fertility plan, with careful monitoring at every step.

Who may benefit from IUI?

  • ✓Couples with unexplained infertility
  • ✓Mild male factor — slightly low sperm count or movement
  • ✓Ovulation problems such as PCOS, combined with ovulation medicines
  • ✓Problems with cervical mucus
  • ✓Difficulties with intercourse
  • ✓Mild endometriosis, in selected cases

When IUI is not suitable

  • Both fallopian tubes are blocked.
  • Very low sperm count or very poor sperm movement.
  • Severe endometriosis or significant damage to the tubes.
  • Several well-performed IUI cycles have not worked.

In these situations, Dr. Jawaria will explain other options such as IVF/ICSI and guide your referral.

Tests before starting IUI

  • Pelvic ultrasound to check the uterus and ovaries.
  • Tubal test (such as HSG) to make sure at least one tube is open.
  • Semen analysis to check count, movement and shape.
  • Hormone tests as needed, including thyroid and prolactin.

The steps of an IUI cycle

1. Ovulation support

Tablets or injections may be started early in the cycle to help one or two follicles grow.

2. Follicle monitoring

Ultrasound scans every few days measure the follicles and the lining of the uterus.

3. Trigger injection

When the follicle is ready, an injection may be given to time ovulation, usually 24–36 hours before IUI.

4. Sperm preparation

The husband’s semen sample is washed in the lab to select the most active sperm.

5. Insemination

The sperm are placed into the uterus with a thin, soft catheter. It takes a few minutes and feels similar to a Pap smear.

6. Pregnancy test

About two weeks later. Progesterone support may be advised in between.

After the IUI procedure

You can rest for a short while and then go home. Most women return to normal daily activities the same day. Mild cramping or light spotting can occur and usually settles quickly.

Take any medicines exactly as prescribed and avoid starting new medicines without advice. If the pregnancy test is negative, Dr. Jawaria will review the cycle with you and plan the next step.

Chances of success vary with the woman’s age, the cause of infertility, the sperm quality and other factors. Several cycles may be needed, and your doctor will discuss realistic expectations with you.

Natural cycle or stimulated IUI?

In a natural cycle IUI, no fertility medicine is used; the clinic tracks your own ovulation and times the IUI. In a stimulated IUI, tablets or injections help one or two follicles grow, which can improve the chance of success in some couples but needs careful monitoring to avoid multiple pregnancy. Dr. Jawaria will recommend the approach that suits your test results.

Preparing for the day of IUI

  • The husband should usually avoid ejaculation for 2–5 days before the sample, as advised by the clinic.
  • The sample is collected in a sterile container provided by the lab and delivered within the time given.
  • The wife does not need to fast. A comfortable, relaxed visit is best.
  • Bring your scan reports and prescription so the timing can be checked.

When to seek urgent care: Contact the clinic or a hospital if you have severe abdominal pain or swelling, fever, heavy bleeding, or shortness of breath after fertility treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Is IUI painful?

Most women feel little or no pain — it is similar to a Pap smear. Mild cramps for a short time are possible.

How long does the IUI procedure take?

The insemination itself takes only a few minutes. Including preparation, plan to be at the clinic for an hour or two.

Can I go back to normal activities afterwards?

Yes. Most women return to their usual routine the same day.

How many IUI cycles are advised?

This depends on your age and test results. Many couples try up to three or four cycles before moving to another option, but your doctor will review progress after each cycle.

What is the difference between IUI and IVF?

In IUI, sperm are placed in the uterus and fertilisation happens naturally inside the body. In IVF, eggs are collected and fertilised in a laboratory, and an embryo is transferred to the uterus.
