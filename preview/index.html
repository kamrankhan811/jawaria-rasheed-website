Fertility & IUI

Trying to conceive? Start with clear answers.

Fertility concerns can feel overwhelming. Dr. Jawaria evaluates both partners, then recommends the simplest effective step — from ovulation support and follicle monitoring to IUI. When another option such as IVF is more suitable, she will explain why and guide you.

Learn about IUI

Your fertility journey

  • ✓1. Consultation for both partners
  • ✓2. Tests: hormones, ultrasound, semen analysis
  • ✓3. A personalised plan, explained clearly
  • ✓4. Treatment such as ovulation induction or IUI
  • ✓5. Follow-up and support at every step
