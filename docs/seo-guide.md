# SEO, AEO and GEO guide — ranking in Bahawalpur and South Punjab

No website can be *guaranteed* the top spot on Google. Rankings depend on the website **plus** Google Business Profile, reviews, links from other sites and time. This site is built to give Dr. Jawaria the strongest possible start. The steps below do the rest.

## Already built into the website

| Area | What's done |
|---|---|
| **On-page SEO** | Each service page has its own SEO title and description with "Bahawalpur" (e.g. *IUI Treatment in Bahawalpur*), one H1, clear H2 sections and 700–1,000 words of content. Pages link to related services, and every page has a "Book" button. |
| **Local SEO** | "Areas served" (Bahawalpur, Multan, Rahim Yar Khan, Bahawalnagar, Lodhran, Vehari, Ahmedpur East, Hasilpur, Khanpur, Liaquatpur) appears on the Home and Contact pages and in the schema. You can edit the list in **Settings → Clinic Info**. |
| **Schema (structured data)** | Physician + MedicalClinic (address, phone, specialties, areas served, social profiles); MedicalWebPage on service pages; BreadcrumbList; **FAQPage** built automatically from each page's FAQ section; AggregateRating only when a real Google rating and review count are entered. |
| **AEO (answer engines, Google AI Overviews, voice)** | Every service page opens with a direct, plain-language answer ("IUI is…"), followed by question-style headings and 5 FAQs each. A separate FAQ page also gets FAQ schema. |
| **GEO (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot)** | **`/llms.txt`** gives AI tools a clean summary of the doctor, clinic, services and links. The site states consistent facts, credits an expert author and shows content as reviewed by the doctor. |
| **Technical** | Clean URLs (`/services/iui-treatment/`), mobile-first responsive design, lazy-loaded images, no heavy sliders, sitemap line in robots.txt, and appointment and review records kept out of search. |

## After the site goes live (do these in order)

1. **Rank Math SEO (free).** Run the setup wizard and choose "Website type: Small business / Medical". Keep **Local SEO** off, because the clinic schema already comes from our plugin. Turn on the sitemap.
2. **Google Search Console.** Verify the domain and submit `https://yourdomain/sitemap_index.xml`.
3. **Google Business Profile (most important for local ranking).**
   - Category: *Gynecologist* (secondary: *Fertility clinic*, *Obstetrician-gynecologist*).
   - Use exactly the same name, address and phone as the website ("NAP" consistency).
   - Add photos, timings and services, and put the website link and appointment link on the profile.
   - Ask happy patients to leave a Google review, and reply to every review.
4. **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Import from Search Console in one click. This also helps ChatGPT and Copilot, which use Bing.
5. **Medical directory listings (citations).** Create or claim profiles on Marham, oladoc and Sehat, with the same NAP and a link to the website.
6. **Social profiles.** Use Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok with the same name and the website link, and add those links in **Settings → Clinic Info**.
7. **Content.** Publish one helpful article a week in **Posts** (for example "IUI success factors", "PCOS diet tips", "First pregnancy scan"). Link each article to the matching service page.
8. **Videos.** Short YouTube or TikTok explainers from Dr. Jawaria. Add the links in Clinic Info so they appear on the site.
9. **Page speed.** Use LiteSpeed Cache (or your host's cache), upload compressed images (WebP, under 200 KB), and test with PageSpeed Insights.
10. **Check monthly.** In Search Console, look at which searches bring visitors, then improve those pages.

## Medical-site rules Google pays attention to (E-E-A-T)

- Show the doctor's real qualifications, registration (PMDC number if she agrees) and experience on the About page.
- Keep the medical content accurate and reviewed by the doctor, and update the "last reviewed" dates.
- Only publish real reviews (with the patient's permission) and real numbers. Never invent success rates.
